Subject: Turn approved project updates into a trustworthy weekly status report with AI

Preview text: A 30-minute lesson for reporting progress, blockers, risks, decisions, and next steps without inventing completion or forecasts.

# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

A weekly status report should help people understand what changed, what needs attention, and what happens next. Instead, teams often spend hours collecting updates and still end up with vague phrases such as “on track” or “almost done.”

AI can organize approved task records, owner updates, decisions, and risks into a readable draft. The valuable skill is producing a **source-backed status report**, not asking AI to make the project look healthy.

## Today's lesson: Report evidence before interpretation

Build the report in two passes:

- **Evidence pass:** Completed work, active work, dates, blockers, decisions, risks, and next actions copied from approved records.
- **Interpretation pass:** Overall health, forecast, escalation, and recommendations supplied or approved by accountable owners.

Label every statement:

- **VERIFIED:** Supported by a current task record, decision, deliverable, or owner update.
- **OWNER FORECAST:** A future estimate supplied by the accountable owner.
- **POTENTIAL RISK:** A concern that still needs validation.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** Information required before a conclusion can be made.
- **DECISION NEEDED:** An authorized person must decide or remove a blocker.

If the evidence does not support a green, amber, or red status, write `STATUS NOT YET CONFIRMED`.

## Try this in 30 minutes

Use a fictional project or records you are authorized to process. Do not upload confidential client information, credentials, personal data, contracts, or regulated material to an unapproved AI service.

1. Choose a fixed reporting period, such as Monday through Friday.
2. Export or copy the approved task list, decision log, risk register, milestone plan, and owner updates for that period.
3. Give every source a name, owner, last-updated date, and link or section reference.
4. Mark stale, conflicting, ownerless, or missing records before asking AI to summarize them.
5. Ask AI to extract completed work only when the source records it as complete.
6. Separate current issues from future risks and ordinary unfinished work.
7. Require an owner, evidence, impact, and next action for every blocker.
8. Use owner-provided dates for forecasts; never let AI calculate certainty from tone.
9. Review the draft with task owners and the project lead, then record corrections.
10. Publish the approved version with its reporting period, source cutoff, owner, and next update date.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Act as a cautious project-status editor. Use only the approved project records
below. Create a DRAFT weekly status report for human review.

Report sections:
1. reporting period and source cutoff
2. executive summary
3. verified achievements
4. work in progress
5. milestones and owner forecasts
6. current issues and blockers
7. potential risks
8. decisions needed, decision owner, and needed-by date
9. next-period actions, owners, and confirmed dates
10. missing, stale, or conflicting information
11. source register

Label each statement VERIFIED, OWNER FORECAST, POTENTIAL RISK,
OPEN QUESTION, or DECISION NEEDED.

Rules:
- do not invent completion, progress percentages, dates, owners, causes, or impact
- do not mark work complete because a message sounds optimistic
- do not convert unfinished work into a blocker without evidence
- do not convert a possible risk into a current issue
- do not assign green, amber, or red health unless the project's approved
  criteria and accountable owner support it
- preserve disagreements and conflicting dates
- show STATUS NOT YET CONFIRMED when evidence is insufficient
- cite the source and last-updated date for every important claim
- do not send, publish, update tasks, or escalate anything

Approved project records:
[Insert authorized task records, decisions, risks, milestones, and updates]
```

## Human review checklist

- The reporting period and source cutoff are explicit.
- Each source is authorized, current, and linked or cited.
- Completed work is supported by completion evidence.
- Progress percentages use an approved method rather than AI judgment.
- Issues, blockers, risks, assumptions, and open questions remain distinct.
- Every blocker has an owner, impact, next action, and review date.
- Forecasts came from accountable owners and include assumptions where needed.
- Health status follows agreed criteria and was approved by the project lead.
- Decisions identify the decision owner and needed-by date.
- Sensitive information is limited to the intended audience.
- Corrections are recorded before distribution.
- The report has an owner, version, approval status, and next update date.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer an **AI-Assisted Weekly Status Report Pack** for one small, low-risk project. Deliver a source register, evidence table, draft report, blocker and decision list, stale-data checklist, reviewer correction log, and reusable template.

The client supplies approved records, status criteria, owners, forecasts, and distribution approval. You organize and draft the report; you do not certify completion, choose project health, revise official records, or send communications without authorization.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Turn one current task list and owner-update set into a cited weekly draft.
- **Medium:** Build recurring templates, source cutoffs, status criteria, decision and risk registers, reviewer workflows, dashboards, and controlled distribution.
- **Hard:** Design governed portfolio reporting with data integrations, identity and access controls, metric definitions, financial and capacity data, audit history, anomaly detection, executive views, and monitored AI agents.

## Free-first learning path

1. Practice with a fictional project in a free document or spreadsheet.
2. Create columns for source, owner, last update, evidence status, and reviewer.
3. Draft the report manually before automating collection or distribution.
4. Ask a second person to trace every important claim back to its source.
5. Complete Microsoft's free beginner lesson on using generative AI responsibly at work.
6. Complete IBM SkillsBuild's free Project Management Fundamentals digital credential.
7. Add paid reporting software only after the evidence and approval workflow is reliable.

## The takeaway

AI can make weekly reporting faster and clearer. Trust comes from current sources, explicit labels, real owner forecasts, visible gaps, and human approval before the report becomes the project's official story.
