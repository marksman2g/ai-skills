## Today’s lesson: Track the full value of one AI workflow

Choose one repeated task, such as drafting follow-up emails, sorting inquiries, or summarizing approved notes. Measure the manual version first, then compare it with the AI-assisted version.

Track these five numbers:

1. **Volume:** How many items were completed?
2. **Total time:** Include setup, prompting, review, corrections, and recovery from mistakes.
3. **Quality:** How many outputs passed review without a major correction?
4. **Full cost:** Include subscriptions, usage fees, setup, training, maintenance, and paid staff time.
5. **Useful result:** Did response time, completed work, error rate, or another meaningful business outcome improve?

Do not count hours “saved” unless that time was actually freed for other useful work. Do not claim revenue growth unless you can connect it to the workflow with reasonable evidence.

## Try this in 30 minutes

Create a worksheet with one row per task and these columns:

`Date | Task | Manual or AI | Minutes | Review minutes | Major correction? | Tool cost | Result notes`

Then:

1. Record five to ten recent manual examples or time a small manual sample.
2. Run the same type of work through an approved AI-assisted process.
3. Include all review and correction time.
4. Compare median minutes per completed item, not just the fastest example.
5. Count outputs that required major correction.
6. Add the tool’s allocated cost for the test period.
7. Decide: keep testing, revise the workflow, or stop.

Use fictional, public, anonymized, or approved information during early tests. Keep customer, employee, payment, health, credential, and confidential data out of unapproved tools.

## Human review checklist

- The manual baseline was recorded before judging the AI result.
- Setup, review, correction, training, and maintenance time are included.
- Tool subscriptions and usage costs are included.
- Quality and error rates are measured alongside speed.
- The same task and completion standard were compared.
- Small samples are labeled as early evidence, not proof.
- Revenue and savings claims can be traced to real records.
- A person decides whether to continue, revise, or stop.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer an **AI Value Tracking Worksheet** for small businesses. Help an owner select one workflow, establish a baseline, record test results, and prepare a plain-language comparison. Avoid guarantees, inflated annual projections, or financial advice. Your value is organizing evidence, not promising a return.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Time one manual task and one AI-assisted task, including review.
- **Medium:** Build a reusable worksheet with quality, error, volume, and cost measures.
- **Hard:** Design controlled pilots with baselines, attribution rules, total-cost models, risk measures, dashboards, and documented investment decisions.

## The takeaway

The best AI workflow is not the one with the most impressive demo. It is the one that produces a measurable business improvement after every cost, correction, and human review is counted.
