## The Simple Lesson

A good 15-minute AI workflow has five parts:

1. **Task**
   - The repeated work you want to speed up.

2. **Input**
   - The note, email, form, transcript, or facts AI will use.

3. **Prompt**
   - The instruction that tells AI what to prepare.

4. **Review**
   - The checklist a person uses before the output becomes real.

5. **Destination**
   - Where the final result goes: email, document, CRM, spreadsheet, calendar, or task board.

This is simple enough to learn and practical enough to sell.

## Example

Task: turn a rough customer message into a reply draft.

15-minute workflow:

1. Paste the customer message.
2. Paste approved business facts.
3. Ask AI to summarize the customer need.
4. Ask AI to draft a reply using only approved facts.
5. Review name, date, price, promise, and tone.
6. Send only after a person approves it.
7. Save the best version as a reusable template.

Reusable prompt:

```text
Write a clear customer reply using only the approved facts below.
Do not invent prices, dates, availability, refunds, guarantees, or policy details.
If information is missing, ask a question instead of guessing.

Customer message:
[paste message]

Approved facts:
[paste business facts]
```

Review checklist:

- Customer name is correct
- Price or policy is not guessed
- Date or availability is verified
- Tone matches the business
- Final next step is clear

## Why This Is Sellable

Small businesses often do not need a large AI rollout first.

They need one visible win:

- Faster replies
- Better follow-up
- Fewer repeated drafts
- Cleaner notes
- More consistent content
- Less scattered admin work

A 15-minute workflow is easy to explain, easy to test, and easy to improve.

## The Skill Ladder

Easy:

- Build one 15-minute workflow
- Write one reusable prompt
- Create one review checklist
- Save one example

Medium:

- Build a small workflow library
- Rank workflows by time saved and risk
- Connect outputs to spreadsheets, inbox labels, or task boards
- Train someone else to use the workflow

Hard:

- Build AI agents
- Connect AI to CRM, calendar, invoicing, and reporting tools
- Create approval gates, logs, and exception routing
- Track performance, cost, accuracy, and privacy

The hard version can earn more, but the first version should be small enough to prove.
