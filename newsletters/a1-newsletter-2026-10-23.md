# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

AI meeting tools can capture discussion, summarize decisions, and suggest action items. The time-saving skill is not pressing **Record**. It is turning the draft into a short, accurate record that tells each person what happens next.

## Today's lesson: Build an action register, not just a summary

Use this four-part structure after an ordinary, approved meeting:

- **Decisions:** What was actually agreed?
- **Actions:** What specific result must be produced?
- **Owners:** Who accepted responsibility?
- **Deadlines:** When is the result due, including the time zone?

AI should mark missing details instead of inventing them. A vague line such as "Jordan will follow up soon" is not a complete action. It needs a deliverable and a confirmed date.

Before recording or transcribing anyone, follow applicable law and organizational policy, tell participants what the tool will capture, obtain any required consent, and confirm where the audio, transcript, notes, and prompts will be stored. Do not place confidential information in an unapproved tool.

## Try this in 20 minutes

Use a fictional transcript or an approved low-risk team meeting.

1. Remove unrelated personal or confidential details.
2. Ask AI to extract decisions, actions, owners, due dates, open questions, and risks.
3. Require a transcript quote or timestamp for every extracted item.
4. Mark any missing owner or deadline as **Unconfirmed**.
5. Compare every item with the source and correct names, numbers, dates, and commitments.
6. Send the draft to attendees with a short correction window.
7. Move confirmed actions into the team's real task system.
8. Apply the approved retention policy to the recording and transcript.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Act as a careful meeting follow-up assistant. Use only the approved transcript below.
Do not invent agreements, owners, deadlines, facts, or intent.

Create:
1. A five-bullet summary
2. A decision log
3. An action table with action, deliverable, owner, deadline, status, and evidence
4. Open questions and risks
5. A concise follow-up email asking attendees to correct errors

For each decision and action, cite the supporting timestamp or exact short excerpt.
Write "Unconfirmed" when the transcript does not clearly name an owner or deadline.
Separate suggestions from commitments. Preserve names, numbers, dates, conditions,
and disagreements exactly. Flag unclear audio or conflicting statements for review.

Approved transcript:
[Paste approved low-risk transcript]
```

## Quality checklist

- Participants received the required notice and gave any required consent.
- The meeting and tool were appropriate for recording or transcription.
- Access and retention settings were checked before capture began.
- Every decision and action has source evidence.
- Suggestions were not mislabeled as commitments.
- Owners and deadlines were confirmed by people, not guessed by AI.
- Names, numbers, dates, conditions, and disagreements match the source.
- Confirmed actions were transferred into the normal task system.
- A human approved the recap before it became the official record.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer an **AI Meeting Follow-Up Pack** for low-risk business meetings. With the client's approved tools and process, organize the transcript, produce a draft decision log and action register, prepare the follow-up email, and track attendee corrections. Sell clarity and follow-through, not a promise that AI captured everything perfectly.

Start with one meeting format and one industry you understand. Avoid legal, medical, employment, financial, disciplinary, safety, or other sensitive meetings unless the client supplies qualified oversight and an approved workflow.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Turn one approved transcript into a reviewed summary and action table.
- **Medium:** Create reusable agendas, consent language, evidence-based prompts, correction workflows, and task-system templates.
- **Hard:** Design organization-wide meeting governance with identity and access controls, retention, eDiscovery, sensitivity labels, integrations, audit trails, quality sampling, and compliance review.

## What's new

Google's August 2026 Workspace update added presentation screenshots to Meet notes, helping connect spoken takeaways with the slide being discussed. Microsoft also documents ways to use Copilot during a Teams meeting without retaining a transcript, although prompts and responses may still be subject to organizational retention and eDiscovery policies. The practical lesson is simple: understand what the tool captures, where artifacts go, and what remains available afterward.

## The takeaway

AI notes become valuable when a person verifies the evidence and turns the conversation into clear responsibility. The final product is not a transcript. It is a trusted action register.
