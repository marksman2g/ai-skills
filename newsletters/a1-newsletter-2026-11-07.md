# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Public reviews are customer service in front of an audience. A good reply acknowledges what the reviewer actually said, protects private information, and gives the next reader confidence that the business pays attention.

AI can help prepare a first draft. It should not investigate the customer, decide who is right, reveal account details, promise compensation, or post without review.

## Today's lesson: Classify before you reply

Place each review into one of four lanes:

- **Appreciation:** Positive feedback that can receive a brief, specific thank-you.
- **Suggestion:** Constructive feedback that should be acknowledged and routed to the right owner.
- **Recovery:** A service problem that needs investigation and a private follow-up path.
- **Escalation:** Safety, threats, discrimination, legal claims, payment disputes, fraud, privacy, medical issues, or other sensitive matters requiring an authorized person.

The lane determines who reviews the response and what the public reply may safely say.

## Try this in 25 minutes

Use fictional reviews or public reviews from a business you are authorized to support.

1. Copy the review text and remove unnecessary personal details before using an AI tool.
2. Record the platform, review date, star rating, and exact topics mentioned.
3. Classify the review into appreciation, suggestion, recovery, or escalation.
4. Identify what can be acknowledged publicly and what must remain private.
5. Ask AI for one short draft using only the review and approved business facts.
6. Require AI to flag accusations, compensation requests, sensitive details, and unclear claims.
7. Add one specific detail that proves a person read the review.
8. Check tone, privacy, accuracy, and the proposed next step.
9. Route recovery and escalation cases to the authorized owner before posting.
10. Publish manually, record the response, and track any operational follow-up separately.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Act as a cautious public-review reply assistant. Use only the review and approved
business information below. Do not decide who is right, investigate identity,
invent facts, reveal customer records, admit legal liability, promise refunds or
compensation, dispute charges, or describe private conversations.

First return:
- lane: APPRECIATION, SUGGESTION, RECOVERY, or ESCALATION
- topics explicitly mentioned
- sensitive or uncertain details
- required human owner
- whether a public draft is appropriate

If a public draft is appropriate, write 40-80 words that:
- acknowledges one specific point from the review
- sounds calm, respectful, and natural
- avoids repeating sensitive details
- does not use canned praise or defensive language
- gives only an approved next step

If the review requires investigation or specialist judgment, do not draft a
substantive answer. Write a neutral holding reply and label it HUMAN APPROVAL
REQUIRED. End with a checklist of every statement the business must verify.

Approved business facts and response rules:
[Paste approved information]

Review:
[Paste the de-identified review]
```

## Human review checklist

- The responder is authorized to speak for the business.
- The reply addresses the actual review rather than only the star rating.
- No customer identity, order, account, health, payment, or private conversation is exposed.
- The response does not invent an investigation result or contradict known facts.
- The wording is respectful and not argumentative, sarcastic, or retaliatory.
- Apologies and acknowledgments do not make unauthorized legal admissions.
- Refunds, discounts, replacements, and other remedies were approved separately.
- Sensitive cases went to the correct human owner.
- The private contact route is current and appropriate.
- The operational issue is tracked outside the public reply and not considered solved merely because someone responded.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer a **Human-Reviewed Review Reply Pack**. The client provides public reviews, brand voice, escalation rules, and approved contact paths. You deliver categorized reviews, draft replies for routine cases, an escalation list, a response log, and a short monthly theme summary.

Begin with drafting only. Keep account access, investigation, remedies, and publishing with the business owner until a mature approval process exists.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Categorize public reviews and draft specific, privacy-safe replies for owner approval.
- **Medium:** Build brand voice rules, escalation lanes, service targets, response logs, issue themes, quality checks, and monthly improvement reports.
- **Hard:** Design a governed multi-platform reputation operation with identity controls, case management, privacy review, multilingual quality assurance, analytics, fraud detection, audit logs, and legal or regulated-industry oversight.

## What's current

Google's current Business Profile guidance says verified businesses can publicly reply to reviews, edit or delete replies, and flag reviews that may violate platform rules. Google reviews business replies before posting, and reviewers may be notified and may update their review afterward.

Recent 2026 research discussed by the University of Arkansas also suggests businesses should decide where generative AI belongs in customer communication, what needs human review, how disclosure may affect trust, and which safeguards reduce privacy or credibility problems. AI may save drafting time, but public accountability remains with the business.

## Free-first learning path

1. Read Google's free review-management guidance and official video.
2. Practice with ten fictional reviews across all four lanes.
3. Build the response log and escalation map in a free spreadsheet.
4. Use a free AI tier only with public or properly de-identified information.
5. Require owner approval before posting every draft.
6. Review recurring themes monthly and improve the underlying service, not just the replies.

## The takeaway

The easiest responsible AI review service is not automatic posting. It is careful triage plus a specific first draft that a qualified person can verify quickly.
