# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical AI lesson is:

**Turn a successful prompt into a reusable workflow card.**

Recent small-business guidance keeps pointing toward the same idea: begin with one useful task, keep a person responsible for the result, and measure whether the process actually saves time. Newer AI tools can handle more steps, but a business still needs clear instructions, approved information, and review points.

## What Is a Workflow Card?

A workflow card is a one-page recipe for completing one repeatable task with AI.

It records:

1. The task and desired result
2. The information AI may use
3. The reusable prompt
4. The required output format
5. The human review checklist
6. The success measure

This is easier to train, test, and sell than vague advice about "using AI."

## Build One in 20 Minutes

Choose a repetitive, low-risk task such as:

- Summarizing meeting notes
- Drafting a reply to a routine inquiry
- Turning one article into three social posts
- Comparing vendor quotes
- Creating a weekly task list

Then complete this card:

```text
WORKFLOW NAME:

BUSINESS RESULT:

INPUTS AI MAY USE:

INPUTS AI MUST NOT USE:

REUSABLE PROMPT:

REQUIRED OUTPUT FORMAT:

HUMAN MUST CHECK:

STOP AND ASK FOR HELP WHEN:

TIME BEFORE AI:

TIME WITH AI:

ERRORS OR REWORK:
```

## Reusable Prompt

```text
You are helping with this business workflow:
[name the workflow]

Goal:
[describe the finished result]

Use only these approved inputs:
[list inputs]

Follow these steps:
1. Identify missing information.
2. Complete the task using only the approved inputs.
3. Present the result in this format: [format].
4. Mark every assumption as ASSUMPTION.
5. Mark every item needing review as HUMAN CHECK.

Do not invent names, prices, dates, policies, credentials, customer details, or promises.
Stop and ask a question if required information is missing.
```

## Example: Customer Inquiry Draft

Business result: prepare a courteous reply without sending it.

Approved inputs:

- Customer's message
- Current service list
- Business hours
- Approved price sheet
- Booking link

Human must check:

- Customer name
- Requested service
- Price and availability
- Any promise or deadline
- Tone
- Links

Success measure:

- Draft prepared in under five minutes
- No invented facts
- Owner makes only minor edits

## Why This Is Sellable

Beginner service offer:

**AI Workflow Starter Pack**

Deliverables:

- One process interview
- Three workflow cards
- Three tested prompts
- Human review checklists
- Before-and-after time estimate
- Short staff handoff session

Learning curve: easy to medium

Effort: approximately two to four hours after practice

Best buyers: local service businesses, solo professionals, nonprofits, creators, real-estate teams, shops, and small offices.

Why a buyer pays: reusable instructions reduce repeated setup, inconsistent output, and training time.

Do not promise a specific income or percentage of time saved. Test the workflow with the buyer's real examples and report the measured result.

## Skill Ladder

Easy:

- Write clear prompts
- Define an output format
- Add a fact-check list
- Save and reuse the workflow card

Medium:

- Map a process from start to finish
- Test several examples and edge cases
- Create a shared prompt library
- Track time saved and correction rates

Hard:

- Connect workflows to forms, email, CRM, or spreadsheets
- Add permissions, logs, and approval stages
- Build multi-step agents with failure recovery
- Monitor quality, cost, privacy, and business outcomes

## This Week's Action

Do not build five workflows. Build one.

Run it three times with three different examples. After each run, record:

- Minutes saved
- Mistakes found
- Instructions that were unclear
- Edits a person still had to make

Improve the card only after seeing what failed. That testing habit is the real skill.

## A1 Takeaway

A clever prompt is useful once. A tested workflow card can be taught, repeated, measured, and sold.

Keep the first workflow small. Keep a person responsible for the final result. Reuse what works.
