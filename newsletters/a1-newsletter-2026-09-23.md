# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical lesson is: **give AI only the information it needs for the task.**

Recent small-business privacy guidance repeats a simple rule: an AI chat is not automatically a private filing cabinet. Before sharing business material, decide what the task requires and remove everything else.

## The Five-Question Check

Before pasting text into an AI tool, ask:

1. Does this task need the person's real name?
2. Does it need their email, phone number, address, account number, or ID?
3. Does it contain employee, health, financial, legal, password, or payment information?
4. Is this the approved business account and tool for this information?
5. Could I complete the task with placeholders instead?

If a detail is unnecessary, remove it. If you do not know whether the tool or data is approved, stop and ask the business owner or responsible person.

## Try This Today

Take one old customer email and make a practice copy. Replace private details:

```text
Maria Johnson -> [CUSTOMER]
maria@example.com -> [EMAIL]
555-123-4567 -> [PHONE]
Order 88347 -> [ORDER NUMBER]
$428.19 -> [AMOUNT, only if unnecessary]
```

Then ask AI to summarize the request or draft a reply using only the redacted copy. Verify the result yourself. Do not upload real sensitive information just to practice.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
Review the text below for a routine business task.

First, list any personal, financial, health, legal, security, credential,
customer, employee, or confidential business information that should be
removed or replaced before the text is used with an AI tool.

Do not repeat sensitive values in your answer. Refer to each by category.
Then provide a redaction template using placeholders such as [CUSTOMER],
[DATE], [AMOUNT], and [ACCOUNT].

Text:
[use a fictional or already approved example]
```

Important: this prompt is a learning aid, not a substitute for the business's privacy rules, contracts, security staff, or qualified legal advice.

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer an **AI-Safe Prompt Preparation Checklist**.

Deliverables: one-page data rules, an approved-placeholder list, five redacted practice examples, and a staff review checklist.

Learning curve: easy to understand, medium to deliver responsibly. Start with public or fictional information. Do not claim to provide legal compliance or security certification.

## Skill Ladder

- Easy: recognize obvious personal details and use placeholders.
- Medium: classify business information and document approved tools and accounts.
- Hard: map data flows, permissions, retention, deletion, vendors, and incident response with qualified specialists.

## Takeaway

Better AI use is not about sharing more. It is about sharing the minimum information required for a clearly defined task.
