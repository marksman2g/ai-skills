Hello,

Suppose two orders contain 3 and 7 notebooks, and a third contains 15 pens. Notebooks lead by order count, but pens lead by units. A polished chart can answer either question. Today, make sure it answers yours.

## Try it in 10 minutes

1. Make two columns in a blank spreadsheet: **Item** and **Units**. Enter three rows: Notebook / 3, Notebook / 7, and Pen / 15.
2. Give AI this prompt: **“Group these rows by item and sum the units. Show the summary and grand total, then build or describe a bar chart titled Units by item. Flag missing or nonnumeric quantities rather than guessing.”**
3. Check the output by hand: **Notebook = 10, Pen = 15, total = 25.** Numbers of 2 and 1 mean the chart counted rows. Two Notebook bars mean it didn't group them.
4. Change the pen quantity from 15 to 5. If the chart should update automatically, verify that it does. If it doesn't, check the chart's source range and whether the summary uses formulas or static numbers.

If Gemini in Sheets isn't available to you, ask an AI tool for instructions and build the chart manually. You don't need a paid spreadsheet AI feature for the exercise. Practice with fictional data and keep private records out of unapproved tools.
