# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's A1 AI lesson is:

**Give AI one source of truth before you ask it to help with business work.**

Recent small-business AI coverage is getting more practical. The same pattern keeps showing up: AI can help with lead follow-up, customer support, scheduling, reporting, invoicing, content, and inbox triage. But it creates problems when the facts are scattered across emails, notes, spreadsheets, websites, and someone's memory.

If AI does not know which information is official, it may sound confident and still be wrong.

That is why one of the most useful beginner AI skills is simple:

```text
Gather the approved facts before asking AI to draft, summarize, or automate.
```

## The Simple Lesson

Before using AI on a business task, create a small source-of-truth sheet.

It can include:

- Business name
- Services
- Prices or approved price language
- Hours
- Booking link
- Policies
- Contact details
- Tone guidelines
- What AI must never invent
- When to ask a human

This turns AI from a guesser into a helper.

## Use This Prompt

```text
Act like an AI workflow setup assistant for a small business.

I want to create a source-of-truth sheet for this repeated task:
[describe task]

Create a simple one-page source-of-truth template with:
1. Facts AI is allowed to use
2. Facts AI must never guess
3. Questions AI should ask when information is missing
4. Human approval rules
5. A reusable prompt that tells AI to use only approved information
6. A checklist before the final output is sent or published
```

## Example

Task: customer reply for a service business.

Source of truth:

- Business hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
- Booking link: approved scheduling page
- Services: basic consultation, premium consultation, monthly support
- Price language: "starting at" language only unless the owner confirms exact price
- Policy: no same-day emergency bookings
- Tone: warm, clear, professional

Reusable prompt:

```text
Write a customer reply using only the approved business facts below.
Do not invent prices, dates, availability, refunds, guarantees, or policy details.
If information is missing, ask a question instead of guessing.

Customer message:
[paste message]

Approved business facts:
[paste source-of-truth sheet]
```

Human review:

- Check customer name
- Check service match
- Check price language
- Check date or availability
- Check promise made
- Check tone

## Why This Is Sellable

Small businesses often want AI help, but their information is not organized.

A source-of-truth setup can help them:

- Reply faster
- Reduce wrong answers
- Keep tone consistent
- Train helpers faster
- Avoid accidental promises
- Prepare for future automation or AI agents

This is a strong beginner service because it is useful before any advanced automation is built.

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer:

**AI Source-of-Truth Setup**

What you provide:

- One source-of-truth sheet
- One reusable prompt
- One human review checklist
- Three example outputs
- One "AI must not guess" rule list
- One recommendation for the next workflow

Learning curve: easy to medium

Effort: low to moderate

Best buyer: small business owners, service providers, nonprofits, creators, local shops, coaches, and anyone using AI with scattered business facts.

Why people pay: they want AI to save time without giving customers wrong information.

## The Skill Ladder

Easy:

- Organize approved facts
- Write prompts that restrict guessing
- Build reply templates
- Create review checklists

Medium:

- Build source-of-truth sheets for multiple tasks
- Create a small AI operations playbook
- Connect facts to spreadsheets, docs, or task boards
- Train a helper to use the workflow

Hard:

- Build an AI knowledge base
- Connect AI agents to business systems
- Add approval gates and logs
- Track accuracy, cost, privacy, and workflow performance

The advanced work pays more because it supports larger systems. But the beginner version is the foundation.

## Try This Today

Pick one repeated task:

- Customer inquiry
- Quote follow-up
- Appointment request
- Social media caption
- Weekly report
- FAQ response
- Invoice reminder

Then create this:

```text
Task:

Approved facts AI can use:

Facts AI must never guess:

Questions AI should ask:

Human approval required when:

Reusable prompt:

Review checklist:
```

If you can make this for one task, you have a practical AI asset.

## A1 Takeaway

AI does not need more scattered information. It needs clearer approved information.

Before you automate the workflow, organize the facts.

That is one of the easiest AI skills to learn, sell, and build on.
