# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's A1 AI lesson is:

**Estimate the 90-day payoff before you automate a workflow.**

Current small-business AI guidance is becoming more practical and less tool-focused. A current AI automation lab for small business owners is built around workflow teardowns, specific automation paths, and a rough ROI estimate for the next 90 days. Recent guides from GoDaddy, Fabren, NetSys, DevLab Studios, and NextTeammate point to the same idea:

```text
Pick one repeated workflow, estimate the payoff, keep humans in the review loop, and measure whether the work actually improves.
```

That is a useful beginner skill because business owners do not only want AI ideas.

They want to know whether the work is worth doing.

## The Simple Lesson

Before automating anything, answer four questions:

1. How often does this task happen?
2. How long does it take manually?
3. What mistakes or delays does it create?
4. What would improve if AI handled the first pass?

Then estimate the 90-day payoff.

You do not need perfect math. You need a reasonable business case.

## Use This Prompt

```text
Act like an AI workflow ROI coach for a small business.

I want to evaluate this repeated workflow:
[describe workflow]

Help me estimate the 90-day payoff.

Ask for or estimate:
1. How often the workflow happens each week
2. Minutes spent each time
3. Who does the work
4. What errors, delays, or missed follow-ups happen
5. What AI could prepare, draft, summarize, sort, or route
6. What a human must review
7. Estimated time saved per week
8. Estimated value over 90 days
9. Risks that might reduce the payoff
10. Whether this is a good first AI workflow
```

## Example

Workflow: lead follow-up.

Manual process:

- 20 lead messages per week
- 10 minutes per message
- Owner reads, summarizes, drafts, checks details, and sends
- Some follow-ups are delayed or missed

AI-assisted process:

1. AI summarizes the lead.
2. AI identifies missing information.
3. AI drafts a reply using approved business facts.
4. AI suggests a follow-up task.
5. Human reviews and sends.

Rough estimate:

- Manual time: 200 minutes per week
- AI-assisted time: 100 minutes per week
- Time saved: 100 minutes per week
- 90-day estimate: about 21 hours saved

That is enough to discuss value clearly.

## Why This Is Sellable

Small businesses often hesitate because AI feels vague.

A 90-day payoff estimate helps them:

- Choose one workflow instead of chasing tools
- See whether the task is worth improving
- Avoid automating low-value work
- Compare time saved against setup effort
- Build confidence before expanding
- Decide what should stay manual

This is one of the easiest AI services to explain because it turns AI into a business decision.

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer:

**AI 90-Day Workflow Payoff Estimate**

What you provide:

- One repeated workflow selected
- Current manual time estimate
- AI-assisted workflow map
- Human review checklist
- Risk and exception notes
- 90-day time-saved estimate
- Recommendation: automate, improve manually first, or leave alone

Learning curve: easy to medium

Effort: low

Best buyer: small business owners, operators, creators, coaches, nonprofits, local service teams, and anyone who wants AI but does not know where it will actually pay back.

Why people pay: they want clarity before spending money on tools, courses, or automation builds.

## The Skill Ladder

Easy:

- Estimate manual time
- List repeated workflows
- Pick one workflow to evaluate
- Write a simple before-and-after

Medium:

- Build a workflow map
- Estimate 90-day payoff
- Create review and exception rules
- Track actual results for one week

Hard:

- Connect AI to CRM, inbox, calendar, invoices, or reporting tools
- Build AI agents with approval gates
- Track ROI, errors, privacy, cost, and adoption over time
- Create an AI rollout plan for a team

The hard work can earn more, but the estimate is what helps the buyer say yes to the right project.

## A1 Takeaway

Do not start with the tool.

Start with the payoff.

A useful AI workflow should save time, reduce errors, improve follow-up, or make work easier to repeat within a clear 90-day window.
