# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical lesson: **use AI to organize meeting notes into an action list, then verify every commitment.**

AI tools are moving into the apps businesses already use. The useful beginner skill is still simple: turn a messy conversation into a short, accurate follow-up. The U.S. Small Business Administration lists meeting summaries as a small-business use case and recommends human review of AI output.

## Try It in 15 Minutes

1. Pick notes from one meeting. Get participants' permission before recording or sharing a transcript. Remove private details before using an unapproved AI tool.
2. Ask AI to separate **decisions**, **action items**, **open questions**, and **background**.
3. Check each action against the original notes. If no owner or date was agreed, leave it blank.
4. Send the checked list to participants for confirmation, not as a record of promises they never made.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
Use only the meeting notes below. Return four short sections:
Decisions made; Action items; Open questions; Background.

For each action item, include task, owner, deadline, and the supporting phrase from the notes.
If the owner or deadline was not stated, write "not assigned" or "not stated."
Do not infer a decision from a suggestion. Do not create commitments.
Flag ambiguous items for human review.

Notes:
[paste approved, de-identified notes]
```

## What to Check

- Is every decision actually stated in the notes?
- Are owners and dates supported by the source?
- Were suggestions incorrectly turned into commitments?
- Is sensitive information omitted from the final email?

## A Service to Practice

Offer a **Meeting-to-Action Template Pack**: a reusable prompt, a one-page recap format, and a review checklist for a small team. Learning curve: easy. Effort: one to two hours to build and test with sample notes. The medium-level step is fitting the process into a team's existing task system, with consent, permissions, and follow-up tracking.

Measure success by minutes saved and corrections found across five meetings. Don't promise flawless transcripts or automatic task assignment.

## Takeaway

AI can organize the notes. People must confirm the commitments.
