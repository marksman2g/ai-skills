# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical lesson is: **use AI to draft a standard operating procedure from a task someone actually performs.**

Small businesses often keep important know-how in one person's memory. AI can make documentation faster, but a polished document is not proof that the process is correct. Start with observation, not a model's guess.

## The 30-Minute Exercise

Choose one low-risk task that happens every week. Perform it once while writing rough notes:

- What starts the task?
- What information or tools are required?
- What steps happen in order?
- What choices does the person make?
- What commonly goes wrong?
- What proves the task is complete?

Remove passwords, keys, customer details, and other private information before using an AI tool.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
Turn my observed notes into a one-page draft SOP.

Include:
1. Purpose
2. Trigger
3. Required inputs and tools
4. Numbered steps
5. Decision points
6. Common exceptions
7. Completion check
8. Process owner and review date

Use only my notes. Do not invent steps, permissions, policies, safety rules,
credentials, approvals, or legal requirements. Mark missing information as
[OWNER TO CONFIRM].

Observed notes:
[paste approved, de-identified notes]
```

## Test the Draft

Ask someone familiar with the task to follow the SOP. Record where they pause, ask a question, use a workaround, or get a different result. Update the document, then have the process owner approve it.

An SOP should also say when to stop and ask for help. Do not use a beginner AI exercise to document safety-critical, regulated, medical, legal, financial, or emergency procedures without qualified review.

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer a **Three-Process SOP Starter Pack**.

Deliverables: three observation interviews, three one-page SOP drafts, owner-confirmation questions, test notes, and revision dates.

Learning curve: easy to medium. The value comes from careful observation and testing, not from generating a long document.

## Skill Ladder

- Easy: capture and format one routine task.
- Medium: document decisions, exceptions, roles, and handoffs.
- Hard: connect approved SOPs to automation, training, audit trails, and controlled change management.

## Takeaway

AI can organize the instructions. The people doing the work must prove they are accurate.
