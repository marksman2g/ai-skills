Subject: A1 AI Skills: Triage the Inbox Before Drafting Replies

Preview text: Sort messages into clear categories first, while keeping sending and sensitive decisions human-controlled.

# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical lesson is: **use AI to classify and summarize messages before asking it to write replies.**

Recent workflow guidance treats inbox work in layers: triage, briefing, then drafting. For a beginner, classification is the lower-risk place to learn because AI can suggest where a message belongs without sending, deleting, moving, promising, or deciding anything.

## Define Five Categories

Use categories that fit the business. A simple service-business version is:

1. New lead
2. Existing customer request
3. Billing or account question
4. Vendor or operations
5. Needs owner review

Write one sentence defining each category. Add a rule that threats, complaints, refunds, contracts, payments, legal notices, health information, passwords, and unusual situations always go to a qualified person.

## Practice Safely

Create ten fictional sample messages. Do not connect an AI tool to a live inbox until the owner has reviewed the vendor, account, permissions, retention, privacy, and security requirements.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
Classify each fictional or approved, de-identified message into exactly one category:
New lead; Existing customer request; Billing/account; Vendor/operations;
Needs owner review.

For each message return:
1. Category
2. One-sentence summary
3. Urgency: normal, soon, or immediate human review
4. Information missing
5. Reason for the classification

Do not draft a reply. Do not infer identity, intent, account status, payment status,
legal meaning, or urgency beyond the text. Route uncertainty and all sensitive or
unusual cases to Needs owner review.

Messages:
[paste fictional or approved, de-identified examples]
```

## Test the Results

Have the person who normally handles the inbox label the same ten examples. Compare disagreements and revise the category definitions. Repeat with new examples until the rules are clear.

Measure classification accuracy, missed urgent items, unnecessary escalations, and review time. Do not claim time savings until the business measures them.

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer an **Inbox Triage Rules Pack**.

Deliverables: category definitions, escalation rules, ten fictional test cases, classification prompt, comparison sheet, and human-review checklist.

Learning curve: medium. The documentation and testing are approachable; connecting a live inbox is higher risk and may expose customer data, attachments, credentials, financial details, or legal notices.

## Skill Ladder

- Easy: design categories using fictional messages.
- Medium: test classification and refine escalation rules.
- Hard: integrate an approved shared inbox with access controls, logging, monitoring, recovery, and qualified privacy and security review.

## Takeaway

Teach AI where a message might belong before asking it to speak for the business.
