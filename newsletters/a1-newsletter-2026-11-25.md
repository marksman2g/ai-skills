# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Automatic captions can turn “fifteen dollars” into “fifty dollars.” Both look plausible on screen. Today’s skill is catching those errors before you share a product demo, class recording, or family video.

## Try it in 15 minutes

1. Pick a one-minute clip you own. Use your editor’s automatic captions or an existing YouTube Studio caption track. Practice with a non-sensitive clip.
2. Listen with the captions on. Check names, prices, dates, units, and words like “not” first. Use a script or approved product sheet to confirm spellings and figures, but caption what was actually said.
3. Add meaningful sounds, such as `[timer beeps]`, and identify speakers when it would otherwise be unclear who is talking. Fix captions that appear too early, disappear too quickly, or cover something important.
4. Watch again muted. Can you follow the instructions and tell who spoke? Save the reviewed caption track and preview it in the actual player.

**Mini test:** Record yourself saying “Set the timer for fifteen minutes, not fifty,” followed by a beep. Check that the caption preserves both numbers, the word “not,” and the beep if it signals the timer started or ended.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Review this draft caption text for names, numbers, units, negations,
and unclear speaker changes. List each line that needs a human check
and explain why. Preserve timestamps and wording. Do not guess what
was said or add sounds you cannot verify.
```

The prompt gives you a review list, not a verified transcript. Listen to the audio before making any correction.

## From practice to paid work

- **Easy:** Proofread one short clip. Learning curve: a few practice clips. A starter offer is caption cleanup for one existing demo, with a reviewed caption file and a playback check.
- **Medium:** Create a client glossary and verify timing, speaker labels, and sound cues across a batch. Allow several sessions to learn the editor and test exports.
- **Hard:** Build a repeatable multilingual review process with qualified language reviewers, version control, and playback checks across platforms. Expect weeks or months of practice, depending on scope.
