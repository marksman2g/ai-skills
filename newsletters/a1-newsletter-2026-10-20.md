Hello,

AI meeting assistants can create useful transcripts, summaries, and action items. They can also turn a private conversation into several stored, searchable records. Today’s lesson helps you plan the entire lifecycle before capture begins.

## Today’s lesson: Treat AI meeting notes as a set of records

Before using an AI notetaker, answer seven questions:

1. **Purpose:** Why is recording, transcription, or AI summarization necessary?
2. **Permission:** What notice, consent, company policy, contract, and local law apply to every participant?
3. **Capture:** Is the tool collecting audio, video, a transcript, speaker identity, chat, screen content, or files?
4. **Storage:** Where are recordings, transcripts, summaries, prompts, exports, backups, and shared links stored?
5. **Access:** Who can view, download, share, or search each artifact?
6. **Review:** Who verifies names, decisions, commitments, owners, and dates before the notes become a record?
7. **Retention:** How long is each artifact kept, and how will deletion be verified?

A visible bot or recording banner may help with notice, but it does not settle every legal, contractual, privacy, employment, or client-confidentiality requirement.

## Try this in 20 minutes

Use a fictional, non-sensitive meeting for practice.

1. Write the purpose and decide whether human notes would be sufficient.
2. Draft an invitation notice naming the tool, what it captures, why, who receives the output, and how long it is kept.
3. Prepare a no-recording alternative for anyone who objects.
4. List every artifact the tool may create.
5. Assign one owner to review the transcript and summary.
6. Correct speaker names, decisions, action owners, deadlines, and unsupported statements.
7. Share only the approved final notes with the intended people.
8. Delete the practice artifacts and confirm what remains in trash, archives, backups, integrations, and vendor accounts.

Do not practice with health, legal, employee, financial, customer, trade-secret, or other sensitive conversations.

## Copy this meeting notice

```text
With everyone’s permission, [organization] would like to use [approved tool] during
this meeting to [transcribe/create draft notes]. It will capture [audio/video/text/
speaker labels] for [specific purpose]. The resulting [recording/transcript/summary]
will be available to [named people or roles] and retained for [period], subject to our
approved policy.

The output may contain errors and will be reviewed by [owner] before it is treated as
a meeting record. Please raise any objection before capture begins or at any time. We
can turn the tool off and use [human notes/other approved alternative].
```

This sample is not legal consent language. Have qualified professionals adapt the process to the participants, locations, contracts, industry, and organization.

## Human review checklist

- Capture is necessary for a defined purpose.
- Notice and required permission occur before capture begins.
- Participants have a practical way to decline or stop capture.
- Unexpected bots or personal recording tools are removed or investigated.
- Sensitive portions can be kept off the recording.
- Names, decisions, commitments, owners, and dates are verified.
- Access is limited to the intended people.
- Retention and deletion are tested across every artifact and integration.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer an **AI Meeting Notes Lifecycle Map** for a small business. Document the owner’s chosen purpose, notice point, artifacts, access roles, review step, retention period, deletion test, and manual fallback. Do not present the map as consent, legal compliance, privacy approval, cybersecurity certification, or vendor assurance without qualified review.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Create a meeting notice, review checklist, and manual alternative.
- **Medium:** Map artifacts, access, retention, deletion, and meeting-type exceptions.
- **Hard:** Govern meeting AI with vendor assessment, contracts, privacy and employment review, admin controls, audit logs, incident response, data-residency requirements, and tested deletion.

## The takeaway

AI meeting notes are not only a convenient summary. Decide what is captured, who agrees, who checks it, who can access it, and when every copy should be removed.
