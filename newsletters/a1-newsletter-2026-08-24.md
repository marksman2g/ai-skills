# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's A1 AI lesson is:

**Make a business easier for AI assistants to find, understand, and recommend.**

People increasingly ask AI tools questions such as:

```text
Who is a reliable plumber near me?
Which local bakery handles birthday cakes?
Where can I find a bookkeeper for a small business?
```

The skill of improving a business's presence in those answers is often called **AI search optimization** or **answer engine optimization (AEO)**. You do not need to code to begin.

## The Simple Lesson

AI answers depend partly on public information about a business. If the website, business profile, directories, and reviews disagree, the answer may be missing or wrong.

Start with four facts:

1. Business name
2. Services offered
3. Service area or location
4. Contact information and hours

Write one approved version of each fact, then compare it with every public source you control.

## Use This Prompt

```text
Act as an AI visibility auditor for a small local business.

Business name: [name]
Location or service area: [area]
Services: [services]
Website text: [paste public text]
Business profile text: [paste public text]

Create:
1. A list of conflicting or missing facts
2. A clear one-sentence business description
3. Five specific customer questions this business should answer online
4. A short FAQ using only the facts provided
5. A checklist of public profiles to review

Do not invent prices, credentials, guarantees, reviews, or service areas.
Mark every missing fact as NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION.
```

## The 30-Minute Exercise

Open the business website and its main public profile. Check whether the name, phone number, hours, address or service area, and main services match.

Then search these five questions in an AI assistant:

```text
What does [business name] do?
Where is [business name] located?
What area does [business name] serve?
When is [business name] open?
How do I contact [business name]?
```

Record each answer in a simple spreadsheet with four columns:

```text
Question | Answer received | Correct? | Source to update
```

Correct the original public source when possible. Do not try to manipulate the AI answer directly.

## Why This Is Sellable

This work connects AI with familiar business needs: accurate listings, clear services, stronger local discovery, and fewer confused customers.

Beginner service offer:

**AI Visibility Accuracy Check**

Deliverables:

- Review of one website and two public profiles
- Ten customer questions tested
- List of missing or conflicting facts
- One approved business description
- Five-question FAQ draft
- Prioritized correction checklist
- Before-and-after answer log

Learning curve: easy

Effort: low, about 60 to 90 minutes for a small business after practice

Best buyers: local service businesses, restaurants, shops, consultants, creators, nonprofits, and solo professionals.

Important boundary: promise an accuracy audit and improvement work, not guaranteed placement in AI answers.

## The Skill Ladder

Easy:

- Check business facts for consistency
- Write clear service descriptions
- Draft useful FAQs
- Test common customer questions

Medium:

- Compare results across several AI assistants
- Track cited sources and corrections
- Build a monthly visibility report
- Improve structured website content with a web professional

Hard:

- Create automated monitoring across locations
- Analyze visibility against competitors
- Connect review, listing, analytics, and CRM data
- Measure whether AI visibility leads to calls, bookings, and sales

The higher-value work comes from measurement and maintenance, but the beginner audit is the right place to learn.

## Safety Check

Use only public, owner-approved business information. Never invent testimonials, certifications, prices, locations, or customer claims. Ask for honest reviews; do not ask customers to stuff reviews with keywords.

## A1 Takeaway

You do not need to build an AI model to offer an AI-related service.

You can help businesses make their public information clear, consistent, and useful so customers and AI systems can understand them.
