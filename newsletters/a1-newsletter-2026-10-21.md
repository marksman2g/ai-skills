# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Clear writing helps customers, coworkers, and family members understand what matters and what to do next. Today’s lesson shows how AI can simplify wording without being allowed to rewrite the underlying facts or obligations.

## Today’s lesson: Lock the meaning before simplifying the words

Before asking AI to revise anything, create a **meaning lock** containing:

- The main message the reader must understand.
- The action the reader should take.
- Facts, names, dates, prices, quantities, and contact details that cannot change.
- Promises, warnings, conditions, exceptions, and required legal or policy wording.
- Terms that must remain because the audience uses or needs them.

Then improve the expression: put the main point first, use familiar words, prefer active voice, keep one idea per sentence, add useful headings, and remove repetition.

AI can make text sound smoother while quietly changing scope or certainty. The final comparison must check meaning, not just grammar.

## Try this in 20 minutes

Choose a short, non-sensitive email, notice, instruction, or website paragraph you are authorized to revise.

1. Name the specific audience and what they need to do.
2. Copy every protected fact and obligation into a meaning-lock list.
3. Highlight jargon, long sentences, hidden actions, and unnecessary background.
4. Ask an approved AI tool for a plain-language draft.
5. Compare the original and revision sentence by sentence.
6. Verify every number, date, name, link, condition, exception, and commitment.
7. Ask one intended reader to explain the message and next step in their own words.
8. Revise from that feedback before publishing or sending.

Do not paste confidential, private, regulated, client-owned, or privileged text into an unapproved AI service.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Act as a plain-language editor. Rewrite the approved text for the audience below so
they can understand the main message and required action on the first reading.

Put the main point first. Use familiar words, active voice, useful headings or bullets,
and one idea per sentence. Remove repetition but preserve every protected fact,
promise, warning, condition, exception, deadline, price, number, name, link, and
required term exactly. Do not add advice, claims, benefits, certainty, or obligations.

After the draft, provide a change table with: original meaning, revised wording, and
verification needed. If simplification might change meaning, write [SUBJECT-MATTER
REVIEW REQUIRED] instead of guessing.

Audience and desired action:
[Describe the reader and what they need to do]

Meaning lock:
[List protected facts and obligations]

Approved original text:
[Paste approved text]
```

## Human review checklist

- The audience and desired action are specific.
- The main message appears early.
- Protected facts and obligations are unchanged.
- Conditions and exceptions were not removed or weakened.
- Familiar words replace jargon where meaning permits.
- Headings, lists, and short paragraphs help readers scan.
- Links, names, numbers, prices, and dates were checked against the source.
- A representative reader tested whether the message is understandable and usable.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer an **AI-Assisted Plain-Language Review** for low-risk business emails, FAQs, instructions, and web copy. Deliver the meaning lock, revised draft, change table, fact-check list, and reader-test questions. Do not claim legal approval, accessibility conformance, translation accuracy, or subject-matter correctness without qualified review.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Simplify one paragraph while preserving a short meaning-lock list.
- **Medium:** Build a repeatable review checklist, style guide, and reader-testing process.
- **Hard:** Lead organization-wide content design with accessibility, multilingual testing, legal and subject-matter review, analytics, governance, and controlled publishing workflows.

## The takeaway

Plain language is not merely shorter writing. It helps a specific reader find, understand, and use information while preserving the truth and every important condition.
