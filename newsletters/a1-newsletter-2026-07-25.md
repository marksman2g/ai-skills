# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's A1 AI lesson is:

**Audit the workflow before you automate it.**

Recent small-business AI guidance is becoming more practical. The strongest advice is not "add AI everywhere." It is to find repeated work, check whether it is safe to automate, measure the time saved, and keep human review where judgment matters.

That is good news if you are learning AI skills.

You do not need to start with a full AI agent. You can start by helping a person or business answer one question:

```text
Which task should we improve first?
```

## The Simple Lesson

Before using AI, score the task.

Use three questions:

1. How often does this task happen?
2. Does it follow similar steps each time?
3. Would a mistake be easy to catch before harm is done?

The best beginner AI workflows are:

- Frequent
- Repetitive
- Easy to review
- Low risk
- Time-consuming enough to matter

## Use This Prompt

```text
Act like an AI workflow auditor for a small business.

Here are repeated tasks in the business:
[paste task list]

Score each task from 1 to 5 for:
1. Frequency
2. Time wasted
3. Repeatability
4. Ease of human review
5. Risk if AI makes a mistake

Then recommend the best first AI workflow to improve.

For the recommended workflow, give me:
- Why it is a good first project
- What AI can help with
- What a human must approve
- A simple prompt template
- A checklist before using the output
```

## Example

Task list:

- Reply to customer questions
- Log invoices
- Make social media captions
- Handle refund requests
- Summarize meeting notes
- Write weekly status updates

Best first workflow:

**Summarize meeting notes into action items.**

Why:

- Happens often
- Low risk
- Easy to review
- Saves time
- Does not require AI to make a final business decision

AI can help:

- Summarize the meeting
- List tasks
- Identify owners
- Pull out dates
- Draft a follow-up message

Human review:

- Confirm names
- Confirm deadlines
- Confirm decisions
- Remove anything AI misunderstood

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer:

**AI Workflow Audit**

What you provide:

- A list of repeated tasks
- A simple scoring table
- The best first AI workflow
- One reusable prompt
- One review checklist
- One before-and-after example

Learning curve: easy to medium

Effort: low

Best buyer: small business owners, nonprofits, coaches, creators, solo operators, and service businesses with repeated admin work.

Why people pay: they do not know where to start, and they do not want to waste money automating the wrong thing.

## The Skill Ladder

Easy:

- List repeated tasks
- Write prompt templates
- Summarize notes
- Draft customer messages

Medium:

- Score workflows by risk and value
- Create SOPs
- Build review checklists
- Connect AI outputs to spreadsheets or task boards

Hard:

- Build AI agents
- Connect tools
- Track AI costs
- Set permissions
- Measure workflow performance over time

The hard version can make more money, but the easy version is the right starting point because it builds trust.

## Try This Today

Make a list of five repeated tasks from your own life or a business you understand.

Then fill this out:

```text
Task:

How often it happens:

Time it takes:

Does it follow the same steps?

What could AI draft, summarize, sort, or compare?

What must a person approve?

Risk level:

Would I sell this as a service?
```

Pick the safest, most repeated task.

That is your first AI workflow sample.

## A1 Takeaway

The easiest AI service to sell is often not building the automation. It is helping someone choose the right workflow to improve first.

Audit first. Automate second. Measure third.
