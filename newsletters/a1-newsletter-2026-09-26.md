# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical lesson is: **use AI to organize customer feedback into themes, then verify the themes against the original comments.**

Recent guides show how AI can help summarize large amounts of feedback. They also warn that AI can miscount, overemphasize a vivid comment, or invent a pattern. The safe beginner workflow keeps every conclusion connected to real feedback.

## Try This With 10 Comments

1. Collect ten public reviews or approved survey comments from one source and one time period.
2. Remove names, emails, order numbers, and other personal information.
3. Read all ten yourself.
4. Ask AI to group them into themes without changing the wording.
5. Check every classification and count against the originals.

Keep different sources separate at first. Mixing support tickets, employee surveys, and public reviews can make the result hard to interpret.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
Analyze the de-identified customer comments below.

For each comment:
1. Assign one or more short theme labels.
2. Mark sentiment as positive, negative, mixed, or neutral.
3. Copy one exact supporting phrase.
4. Flag any comment that is ambiguous.

Then count the checked themes and list three possible business questions.
Do not invent quotes, causes, percentages, customers, or recommendations.
Keep every conclusion traceable to a numbered comment.

Comments:
[paste numbered, approved, de-identified comments]
```

## Human Review Checklist

- Does every quote match the original exactly?
- Does each label fit the whole comment, including mixed feedback?
- Are the counts correct?
- Is one dramatic comment being mistaken for a common pattern?
- Is there enough feedback to justify a decision?

With fewer than 50 comments, read them all. AI is helping you organize, not replacing judgment.

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer a **Customer Feedback Theme Report**.

Deliverables: de-identified comment set, checked theme table, exact supporting quotes, count summary, ambiguous-items list, and three questions for the business owner.

Learning curve: easy to medium. Use public, fictional, or owner-approved feedback. Do not create fake reviews, expose customer information, or claim that a small sample represents every customer.

## Skill Ladder

- Easy: label ten comments and verify the quotes.
- Medium: compare themes by month, location, product, or customer segment.
- Hard: build a governed feedback pipeline with consent, access controls, quality testing, and trend monitoring.

## Takeaway

AI can organize what customers said. The original comments remain the evidence.
