# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Today's practical lesson is: **use AI to reshape strong existing content instead of asking it to invent content from nothing.**

Recent small-team playbooks describe content repurposing as extracting useful ideas from one substantial source and adapting them for different channels. The owner remains the source of the expertise; AI handles repetitive restructuring.

## The 30-Minute Exercise

Choose one owner-approved source:

- A helpful article
- A video or podcast transcript you own
- A webinar
- A recorded explanation with participant permission
- A public FAQ or guide from the business

Select three ideas that can stand alone. For each idea, create one email paragraph, one professional social post, and one 30-second video outline.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
Repurpose the approved source below.

First, extract three distinct ideas and copy the exact supporting passage
for each one. Then adapt each idea into:
1. One email paragraph under 120 words
2. One professional social post under 180 words
3. One 30-second video outline

Use only the source. Preserve the speaker's meaning and stated point of view.
Do not invent stories, results, statistics, customers, quotations, credentials,
or opinions. Mark any missing detail as [OWNER TO ADD].

Source:
[paste approved content]
```

## Human Review Checklist

- Does every new piece trace back to the source?
- Is the meaning unchanged?
- Does the format fit the channel instead of repeating identical text?
- Are all facts, quotes, names, and numbers accurate?
- Does it still sound like the owner?
- Is the call to action relevant and approved?

## Beginner Service Idea

Offer a **One-to-Nine Content Pack**: three ideas from one approved source, each adapted into an email paragraph, social post, and short-video outline.

Deliverables: source map, nine edited drafts, verification checklist, and owner-approval sheet.

Learning curve: easy to medium. Begin with text and outlines. Video editing, licensed media, voice cloning, automated publishing, and paid advertising add substantial consent, copyright, disclosure, brand, and platform-policy responsibilities.

## Skill Ladder

- Easy: extract supported ideas and rewrite for three formats.
- Medium: preserve brand voice and manage an editorial calendar.
- Hard: run a governed multimedia workflow with rights tracking, approvals, analytics, and automation.

## Takeaway

Good repurposing multiplies a real idea. It does not multiply unsupported claims.
