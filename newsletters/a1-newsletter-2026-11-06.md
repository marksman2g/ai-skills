Subject: Write better product descriptions without inventing product facts

Preview text: A free-first workflow for turning approved specifications into clear, useful ecommerce copy that a human can verify.

# A1 AI Skills Newsletter

Hello,

Product-description writing looks easy until a catalog contains dozens of items, inconsistent supplier notes, missing specifications, and old prices. AI can speed up the first draft, but it can also quietly add a material, feature, compatibility claim, or use case that was never approved.

The practical skill is building a clean product fact sheet first, then using AI only to explain those facts clearly.

## Today's lesson: Separate facts from sales language

Create one structured fact sheet per product:

- **Identity:** Product name, model, SKU, and brand.
- **Variant:** Size, color, capacity, pack quantity, or configuration.
- **Materials:** Exact approved composition and finish.
- **Dimensions:** Measurements, units, weight, and tolerances if applicable.
- **Features:** What the product demonstrably has or does.
- **Use:** Approved intended uses and important limitations.
- **Compatibility:** Confirmed systems, accessories, devices, or environments.
- **Care and safety:** Owner-approved instructions and warnings.
- **Commercial facts:** Current price, availability, shipping, warranty, and return source.
- **Evidence:** The source ID and date supporting every important claim.

Do not ask AI to infer hidden specifications from an image. If the source is silent, the draft should be silent too.

## Try this in 25 minutes

Use a fictional item or a product you are authorized to describe.

1. Gather the current manufacturer sheet, approved store page, packaging copy, and owner notes.
2. Resolve conflicts before drafting; do not let AI choose which source is correct.
3. Fill the fact sheet and attach a source ID to each field.
4. Mark missing fields **Unknown - do not claim**.
5. Ask AI for a plain-language description and five factual bullets.
6. Require a claim table showing the source for every statement.
7. Remove filler such as "best," "perfect," "premium," or "guaranteed" unless evidence and approval support it.
8. Compare title, description, variants, price, availability, and landing-page data.
9. Have the product owner review the final text.
10. Publish one item first and check the live listing before scaling to the catalog.

## Copy this prompt

```text
Act as a careful ecommerce copy assistant. Use only the approved product fact
sheet below. Do not infer details from the product name or image. Do not invent
materials, dimensions, compatibility, performance, origin, sustainability,
safety, warranty, availability, customer results, or certifications.

Create:
1. one specific product title
2. one 80-120 word plain-language description
3. five factual feature bullets
4. one concise use-and-limitations note
5. one claim table with claim, source ID, and status

Lead with the details that help the intended buyer decide whether the product
fits their need. Keep features distinct from benefits. Avoid keyword stuffing,
unsupported superlatives, false urgency, and generic hype. Mark every missing
or conflicting field "NEEDS PRODUCT-OWNER REVIEW" and do not use it in the
customer-facing copy.

Approved product fact sheet:
[Paste fields with source IDs and dates]
```

## Human review checklist

- Product identity and variant match the item being sold.
- Every material, measurement, feature, and compatibility claim has a current source.
- Price, availability, shipping, warranty, and returns match the live store systems.
- The title and description refer to the same item shown on the landing page.
- Benefits do not overstate what a feature can deliver.
- Safety, health, environmental, and certification claims received qualified review.
- No copied supplier or competitor text is used without permission.
- Images accurately represent the product and selected variant.
- AI-generated catalog text uses required platform fields or disclosures.
- The product owner approved the final listing and knows how to correct it later.

## A beginner service you could offer

Offer a **Verified Product Description Pack**. The client supplies approved product sources. You deliver a structured fact sheet, title, description, feature bullets, claim table, missing-information report, and one revision after owner review.

Begin with a small batch of low-risk products. Do not write copy for medical devices, supplements, regulated goods, safety equipment, financial products, or other high-stakes items without qualified review and the applicable platform controls.

## Skill ladder

- **Easy:** Turn one clean product fact sheet into accurate, readable copy.
- **Medium:** Build catalog templates, variant rules, brand voice, bulk quality checks, approval workflows, and feed consistency audits.
- **Hard:** Design a governed product-information system spanning suppliers, ecommerce platforms, marketplaces, structured data, inventory, localization, accessibility, compliance, APIs, and change history.

## What's current

Google Merchant Center's current guidance emphasizes accurate, complete, up-to-date product data and consistency between listings and landing pages. Google also provides structured title and description attributes for AI-generated catalog text, so merchants should check current feed requirements instead of treating generated copy as ordinary unlabeled text.

WooCommerce's 2026 guidance similarly recommends clear, specific product descriptions that help both people and AI-powered search understand the item. The durable principle is simple: structured facts first, readable explanation second.

## Free-first learning path

1. Practice with one fictional item and a one-page fact sheet.
2. Use a free spreadsheet to track fields, sources, dates, and approval status.
3. Read Google Merchant Center's free product-data guidance.
4. Use a free AI tier only with non-sensitive approved product information.
5. Compare each generated claim with the original source manually.
6. Test a small batch before paying for bulk catalog-generation software.

## The takeaway

AI can produce product copy quickly. The sellable skill is preventing plausible fiction from reaching the product page.
